🛠️ What I solved

  • Rebuilt a Kaggle T-SQL blueprint in MySQL: staging, clean tables, star schema, views.
  • Fixed payment double-counting by using two fact tables at different grains (per item and per order).
  • Fixed RFM logic: frequency scored with CASE (most customers ordered once), unreachable segment rule, hardcoded date.
  • Found that retention is under 1% a month after first order, so repeat purchase is the real problem.
  • Used window functions: ROW_NUMBER, RANK with PARTITION BY, LAG, NTILE, running totals.
